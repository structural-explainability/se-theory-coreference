known limitations, unresolved questions, and safeguards
against overclaiming for this theory.

## Register 1. Doubts About Assumptions (Undermining Doubts)

### UM-001. (OPEN) Successful Denotation

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

Does a reference denote one intended referent?

#### Current Knowledge

The theory has not established when a reference successfully denotes.
Even with no co-reference links asserted, the generated relation relates each reference to itself.
That does not establish that the reference denotes an existing or uniquely identified referent.

#### Remaining Work

Co-reference results with explicit denotation semantics.

### UM-002. (OPEN) Warrant for Co-Reference Judgments

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

When do separately produced records warrant a positive co-reference judgment, and when is a generated link sound?

#### Current Knowledge

Neither has been established.
Warrant requires independently justified conditions connecting references to intended referents, including reliable attribution and adequate evidence.
The generic commitment calculus does not supply them.

#### Remaining Work

Evidence and assumptions justifying the judgment for an intended referent kind.

### UM-003. (OPEN) Transport Across Contexts

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

When may a co-reference judgment carry from one context to another?

#### Current Knowledge

Not established.
What licenses continuation across contexts remains active research.

#### Remaining Work

Criteria for transport, with their justification.

### UM-004. (OPEN) NIST Disk Chopper Spectrometer Investigation

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

Can two independently produced presentations be shown to identify one physical acquisition episode?

#### Current Knowledge

The October 2026 investigation did not establish this.
The accessible file-derived index record and its source file shared a production lineage, so their agreement did not independently establish occurrence identity.
The investigation was suspended because the necessary evidence could not be inspected, not because occurrence co-reference was shown to be impossible.

#### Remaining Work

Reconsideration requires run-specific evidence, documented record-production and attribution rules, and a test that discriminates competing acquisition histories.
