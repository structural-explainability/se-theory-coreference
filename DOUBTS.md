# Research Doubts and Open Questions

This document records the current research assessment,
known limitations, unresolved questions, and safeguards
against overclaiming for this theory.

For the three research criteria, doubt classifications,
status definitions, and procedures, see the
[Structural Explainability Research Doubts Standard](https://github.com/structural-explainability/.github/blob/main/RESEARCH-DOUBTS-STANDARD.md).

## Repository Summary

This assessment is a summary of the present evidence.

| Criterion                | Current assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Mathematical Correctness | Formal results are established under stated hypotheses. Reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity of generated co-reference are properties of the closure.                                                                                                                             |
| Scientific Adequacy      | Not established. The theory does not establish successful denotation, warrant for co-reference judgments, soundness of generated links, or transport across contexts.                                                                                                                |
| Theoretical Contribution | The proposed contribution is a context-indexed reading of a Neutral Substrate co-reference account through the Persistence commitment calculus: generated co-reference, distinct-commitments, and contextual consistency. No related-work discussion is recorded in this repository. |

The doubts below identify specific issues affecting these assessments.

## Register 1. Doubts About Assumptions (Undermining Doubts)

### UM-001. (OPEN) Successful Denotation

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

Does a reference denote one intended referent?

#### Current Knowledge

The theory has not established when a reference successfully denotes.
Even with no co-reference links asserted, the generated relation relates each reference to itself.
That does not establish that the reference denotes an existing or uniquely identified referent.

#### Remaining Work

Co-reference results with explicit denotation semantics.

### UM-002. (OPEN) Warrant for Co-Reference Judgments

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

When do separately produced records warrant a positive co-reference judgment, and when is a generated link sound?

#### Current Knowledge

Neither has been established.
Warrant requires independently justified conditions connecting references to intended referents, including reliable attribution and adequate evidence.
The generic commitment calculus does not supply them.

#### Remaining Work

Evidence and assumptions justifying the judgment for an intended referent kind.

### UM-003. (OPEN) Transport Across Contexts

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

When may a co-reference judgment carry from one context to another?

#### Current Knowledge

Not established.
What licenses continuation across contexts remains active research.

#### Remaining Work

Criteria for transport, with their justification.

### UM-004. (OPEN) NIST Disk Chopper Spectrometer Investigation

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

Can two independently produced presentations be shown to identify one physical acquisition episode?

#### Current Knowledge

The October 2026 investigation did not establish this.
The accessible file-derived index record and its source file shared a production lineage, so their agreement did not independently establish occurrence identity.
The investigation was suspended because the necessary evidence could not be inspected, not because occurrence co-reference was shown to be impossible.

#### Remaining Work

Reconsideration requires run-specific evidence, documented record-production and attribution rules, and a test that discriminates competing acquisition histories.

## Register 2. Doubts About Conclusions (Rebutting Doubts)

These questions concern whether a claimed conclusion is false.
For example, a counterexample satisfying all the premises
but violating the conclusion would challenge the claimed theorem or its formalization.
A model that violates a required assumption does not
necessarily refute a conditional theorem.
If a counterexample is found,
we must identify the exact claim it challenges and
whether its assumptions are satisfied.

**None currently identified.**

## Register 3. Doubts About What Our Results Mean (Undercutting Doubts)

These questions concern the connection
between a formal theorem and the scientific claim made about it.
A theorem may be correct while its informal interpretation is too strong.
Some of these doubts are addressed by permanent documentation
rules rather than additional proofs.

### UC-001. (STANDING RULES) Generated Co-Reference Versus Referent Identity

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

A reader might read the generated equivalence as identity of intended referents.

#### Current Knowledge

Generated co-reference is the equivalence closure of asserted-same pairs.
Its reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity are properties of that closure.
They do not establish successful denotation or identity of the intended referents.

#### Standing Rules

1. Describe generated co-reference as a closure of supplied judgments.
2. Do not present it as warranted referent identity.

### UC-002. (STANDING RULES) Consistency and Dependent Records

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

A reader might read contextual consistency as validation, or agreeing records as corroboration.

#### Current Knowledge

Contextual consistency establishes compatibility of supplied co-reference and distinct-commitments.
It does not validate either set against the intended referents.
Records may inherit an erroneous attribution from a common source, so agreement between dependent records is not independent corroboration.

#### Standing Rules

1. Do not present consistency as validation of the judgments.
2. Do not treat agreement between dependent records as independent corroboration.

### UC-003. (STANDING RULES) Undetermined Judgments

#### Research criterion

Scientific Adequacy

#### Concern

A reader might want a definite verdict where the evidence gives none.

#### Current Knowledge

A pair that is not co-referent is not thereby established to refer to different wholes.

#### Standing Rules

1. Do not promote an undetermined judgment to co-reference or distinctness merely to produce a definite result.
